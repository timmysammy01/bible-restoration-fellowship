WELCOME HOME

A place to encounter God and grow in faith.

Bible Restoration Fellowship is a community of believers in Ifako-Gbagada, Lagos, gathered around the truth of Scripture and the person of Jesus Christ.

We desire to see people restored spiritually, strengthened in faith, equipped for purposeful living and connected to a loving Christian family.

OUR VISION

Restoring people through the truth of God's Word.

We seek to build a Bible-grounded fellowship where people can know Christ, discover purpose, serve others and become a positive influence in their homes and communities.
